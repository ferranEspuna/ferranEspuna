## About me

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at UAM–ICMAT in Madrid, Spain, under the supervision of Pablo Candela. My research focuses on arithmetic combinatorics, higher-order Fourier analysis, and additive structures. Previously, I was a Research Engineer at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center working on large language models. I enjoy creating mathematical visualizations and exploring the synergies between programming, AI, and mathematics. I completed my M.Sc. in Advanced Mathematics at UPC and double B.Sc. in Mathematics and Computer Science at UB.
